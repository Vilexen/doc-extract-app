# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our
community include:

* Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
* Being open to new ideas and perspectives
- Showing empathy towards other community members
* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
* Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the
  overall community
* Exhibiting empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior by participants include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery and sexual attention or advances
  of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic
  address, without explicit permission
* Assuming others' gender and sexual orientation
* Solicitation or harassment, both online or in-person
- Advocating for, or encouraging, any form of violence
* Conduct that could be considered illegal in local, state, national, or
  international jurisdictions

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards
of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action
in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not
aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate to Banned or
temporarily banned contributors the reasons for their decisions.
Community leaders may also temporarily ban a contributor from the
community's events or online discussions for conduct that they deem
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies
when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.
Examples of representing our community include but are not limited to using
an official community email address, posting via an official community
account, or acting as an appointed representative at an online or offline
event.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the community leaders responsible for enforcement at
conduct@invoiceextractor.com. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.

All community leaders are obligated to protect the privacy and security of
any individual who reports an incident of abusive, harassing, or otherwise
unacceptable behavior.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in
determing the consequences of any action they deem inappropriate,
threatening, offensive, or harmful:

### 1. Correction
**Impact**: Inappropriate or unprofessional behavior that does not cause harm
to an individual or to the community as a whole.

**Consequence**: A private, written warning from community leaders, providing
clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the
behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning
**Impact**: A single incident or series of inappropriate or unprofessional
behaviors that causes harm to an individual or to the community as a whole.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. A
formal apology may be requested, but is not required.

### 3. Temporary Ban
**Impact**: A serious violation of community values, including causing
harm to an individual or to the community as a whole.

**Consequence**: A temporary ban from any sort of community interaction or
public speaking for a period of one week to one month. Community leaders
may also temporarily ban a contributor from the community's events or
online discussions for conduct that they deem inappropriate, threatening,
offensive, or harmful.

### 4. Permanent Ban
**Impact**: A pattern of violating community values, including causing
harm to an individual or to the community as a whole.

**Consequence**: A permanent ban from any sort of community interaction or
public speaking.
